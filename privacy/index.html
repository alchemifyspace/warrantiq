Privacy policy

Draft — last updated August 2026

WarrantIQ is a warranty-tracking app from Alchemify. We collect the account email you sign in with, asset details you enter, and photos you upload (receipts and labels) so we can store them for you and extract warranty fields.

Photos and asset rows are stored in Supabase, scoped to your user id, and protected with row-level security. We do not sell personal data.

AI extraction is sent to a vision model via OpenRouter to fill form fields. Do not upload documents you do not want processed that way.

Questions: use the contact option in the app, or email the publisher listed on the Google Play listing.
